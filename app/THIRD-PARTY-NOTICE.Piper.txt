MediaDroyd reads Piper's voice configs and makes their phoneme ids as Piper does (PiperVoice). The voices are fetched
when chosen from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices at a fixed revision, each file checked against its digest;
each voice's training data carries its own licence, named beside it in the app:

  de_DE thorsten (CC0) — Thorsten-Voice, https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice
  de_DE kerstin (CC0) — https://github.com/rhasspy/dataset-voice-kerstin
  en_US libritts_r (CC BY 4.0) — LibriTTS-R, http://www.openslr.org/141/
  en_GB alba (CC BY 4.0) — https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270
  en_GB cori (CC BY 4.0) — Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS Dataset, NVIDIA Corporation, https://www.openslr.org/109/
  fr_FR siwis (CC BY 4.0) — https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
  es_ES davefx (CC0) — https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
  it_IT paola (CC0) — https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
