Built from its release source (native/whisper; native/build.gradle.kts fetches and checks it) into MediaDroyd's native
library, unmodified, to make subtitles from the speech as a film plays.

The models are OpenAI's Whisper (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI), as whisper.cpp's quantised ggml files from
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp at a fixed revision, fetched when chosen and checked against their digests.
