MIT KEMAR head-related impulse responses (https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html)

core/player/src/main/resources/com/hawkynt/mediadroyd/player/hrir-44100.f32 and hrir-48000.f32 hold the responses
of the normal pinna for nine directions on the horizontal plane, read from libmysofa's SOFA copy
(MIT_KEMAR_normal_pinna.sofa) by tools/hrir/hrir.c; VirtualSurround places 5.1 and 7.1 with them.

Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone", MIT Media Lab Perceptual
Computing Technical Report #280, May 1994.
